Several Ninth Circuit appeals challenged the transfer of Oak Flat, federally owned land in the Tonto National Forest, to Resolution Copper. The consolidated appeals (Nos. 25-5185, 25-5189 and 25-5197) include Arizona Mining Reform Coalition v. U.S. Forest Service, San Carlos Apache Tribe v. U.S. Forest Service, and Lopez v. United States. Our data says a 2-1 panel decision in March 2026 cleared the land exchange; I did not read that opinion.

On September 29, 2026, the court issued an order on petitions for panel rehearing and rehearing en banc. It states: "Judges M. SMITH and BRESS voted to deny the Plaintiffs-Appellants' petitions for panel rehearing and/or rehearing en banc," and "Judge RAWLINSON voted to grant" rehearing en banc. The matter failed to receive a majority of the non-recused active judges in favor of en banc consideration.

Our data adds that eleven judges urged the Supreme Court to correct what Chief Judge Murguia called a "tragic error." I could not verify that statement or the panel opinion. The order leaves the panel decision in place, subject to any Supreme Court petition, and follows the en banc ruling in Apache Stronghold (2024).
