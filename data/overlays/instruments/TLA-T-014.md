On January 31, 1786, U.S. commissioners met Shawnee chiefs and warriors at the mouth of the Great Miami River. The treaty required three hostages to be held until prisoners were returned. The Shawnee acknowledged "the United States to be the sole and absolute sovereigns of all the territory ceded to them" by Britain in the 1783 peace. Article VI allotted them land in southwestern Ohio and the Wabash country, and the Shawnee relinquished "all title, or pretence of title" to lands east, west and south of its lines.

The treaty text shows the United States demanding hostages and warning that a nation that failed to report plans for war "shall be considered as parties in such war." The sources I opened do not describe the negotiation itself in more detail.

The Shawnee were not parties to the Treaty of Fort Harmar in 1789, and Richland Source says many Ohio nations, including the Shawnee, refused to honor it. Greenville in 1795, also in this atlas, replaced the earlier lines.
