On October 17, 1802, at Fort Confederation on the Tombigbee, Brigadier General James Wilkinson made a "provisional convention" with Choctaw chiefs, head men and warriors. Article I let the President retrace and re-mark the old line from the Chickasawhay River to the Tombigbee, with two Choctaw commissioners present. Article II released and quit-claimed land south of that line between the Chickasawhay and the Tombigbee and Mobile rivers.

The text says this was done "freely, voluntarily, and without constraint," and "in consideration of one dollar" paid by the United States. Article III let the chiefs of the upper towns make a change in the old boundary near the Yazoo mouth if it caused no injury to the nation. The convention took effect once ratified.

The sources I opened do not explain why the United States wanted this land or how the council went, and I did not find any acreage. The OSU margin says it was ratified June 20, 1803, after a proclamation date of January 20, 1803, which looks reversed.
