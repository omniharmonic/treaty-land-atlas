On November 16, 1805, James Robertson and Silas Dinsmoor made a "treaty of limits" at Mount Dexter in Choctaw country with the Choctaw mingoes, chiefs and warriors. Article I has the Choctaw cede all lands to which they "now have or ever had claim" lying to the right of described lines, starting near Natchez and running east across the Pearl River and along the Chickasawhay toward the Tombigbee, with a reservation for the Choctaw named in the text.

The United States agreed to pay $50,500: $48,000 "to enable the Mingoes to discharge the debt due to their merchants and traders" and to cover property losses, and $2,500 to John Pitchlynn. It also promised $3,000 a year in goods, $500 each to three "great Medal Mingoes," and $150 yearly annuities to each during their time in office.

A web search suggested sources differ on the role of Pushmataha, who was one of the three named chiefs, and on how many Choctaw attended. I did not open a source, so I make no claim about that.
