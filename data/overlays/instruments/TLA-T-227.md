On October 21, 1837, in Washington, Carey A. Harris and the Sacs and Foxes of Missouri signed a treaty. The Missouri Sac and Fox released all their right or interest in the country between the Missouri and Mississippi rivers, up to the Sac and Fox-Sioux boundary described in the 1825 treaty, and gave up claims under several earlier treaties for which no appropriations had been made.

The United States agreed to pay $160,000. Of that, $2,500 went for merchandise and $100 to their interpreter, and $157,400 was to be invested at not less than 5 percent. The interest paid for a blacksmith, a farmer, a teacher and school, and a yearly payment of $4,500 in money or merchandise. The United States also paid the cost of the delegation.

The text releases claims rather than describing a physical removal. The sources I read do not trace later events for this band.
