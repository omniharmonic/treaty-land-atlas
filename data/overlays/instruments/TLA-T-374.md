On August 13, 1868, at Washington, Commissioner Nathaniel G. Taylor and three Nez Perce chiefs, Lawyer, Timothy and Jason, signed amendments to the June 9, 1863 treaty. The preamble says the tribe desired amendments and the United States was willing to assent.

Article 1 directed that farmable lands within the 1863 reservation be surveyed and allotted. Nez Perce living outside the reservation could be moved onto allotments inside it, or, if there was not enough land, remain on land they already occupied, up to twenty acres per adult male, protected by the military. Article 2 annulled the 1863 clause letting the United States use timber for forts, and required the head chief's written consent to cut timber. Article 3 required Congress to repay school and teacher money not spent for that purpose since 1864.

The amendments were signed by the same pro-treaty chiefs as in 1863. The pages opened here do not describe the non-treaty bands' response.
