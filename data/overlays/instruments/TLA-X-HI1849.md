This is a treaty between the United States and the King of the Hawaiian Islands, not a treaty with a Native nation inside the United States. It was signed at Washington on December 20, 1849, by Secretary of State John M. Clayton and James Jackson Jarves, accredited as the King's special commissioner.

Article 1 provides for perpetual peace and amity between the United States and the King, his heirs and successors. Article 2 sets reciprocal liberty of commerce and navigation, with no higher customs duties on one country's goods than on any other country's, and a most-favored-nation clause. The Statutes at Large page records that ratifications were exchanged at Honolulu on August 24, 1850, and that the treaty was proclaimed on November 9, 1850.

The sources I read do not state why the United States sought the treaty. The treaty is relevant to the atlas because it treats the Kingdom as a state in treaty relations with the United States. The later overthrow and annexation (1893 and 1898) are not covered by the sources I opened.
