Apache Stronghold describes itself as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit community organization based in San Carlos, Arizona. Its website says it connects Apaches and other Native and non-Native allies to defend holy sites and freedom of religion. ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer lists it under EIN 47-4032813 with tax exemption issued in December 2020.

This group does land defense, not land return. Its central cause is Oak Flat, which it calls Chi'chil Bildagoteel, federal land inside the Tonto National Forest. Apache Stronghold says a land-exchange rider attached to the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2015 became law on December 19, 2014. It says the exchange gives the area to Resolution Copper for a large copper mine. The group says it is fighting to repeal the exchange.

Apache Stronghold's own press page records the legal fight. It lists a May 27, 2025 post headed "Supreme Court refuses plea to protect Oak Flat" and a separate post that includes the dissents of Justice Gorsuch and Justice Thomas. Its posts also record a federal judge blocking the land exchange in May and August 2025 and an October 6, 2025 post saying the Supreme Court took no action.

All of this is the organization's account of events.
