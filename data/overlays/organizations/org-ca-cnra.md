The California Natural Resources Agency (CNRA) is a state government agency, not a charity or a Native organization. This record covers it as a funder of tribal land return through the Tribal Nature-Based Solutions program.

The state's Wildfire and Forest Resilience Task Force page says that in May 2024 the agency, with CAL FIRE and the Ocean Protection Council, awarded $107.7 million to fund 33 projects. It says the awards support the return of approximately 38,950 acres to California Native American tribes through the grant program. The page says the money also funds habitat restoration, wildfire resilience and cultural fire. Other coverage gives slightly different project counts, so treat the number of projects as approximate.

One result is in Butte County. Hoodline, citing local station Action News Now, reports that the Mechoopda Indian Tribe of Chico Rancheria acquired about 450 acres in the Big Chico Creek watershed in 2026, with state programs supporting the closings. A real estate firm that worked on the deal says a program grant funds the tribe's cultural and environmental restoration crew.

CNRA's own press release on the Mechoopda return could not be opened in this session. The Mechoopda detail therefore rests on secondary reports. This record does not describe the program's current funding.
