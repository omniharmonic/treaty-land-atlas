The Cultural Fire Management Council (CFMC) describes itself as "a community based non-profit organization, furthering fire use and cultural resource management." Its site is headed Weitchpec, California. ProPublica lists it in Hoopa, California, under EIN 47-5001679, with tax exemption issued in September 2015. Both places are on or near the Yurok Reservation.

CFMC's stated mission is "to facilitate the practice of cultural burning on the Yurok Reservation and Ancestral lands." It says this leads to a healthier ecosystem, long-term fire protection for residents, and support for traditional hunting and gathering by the Yurok. Margo Robbins is listed as executive director, and Ron Reed as board president.

The site lists programs including family burns, a Training Exchange (TREX), a fuels reduction program, cooperative burns, plant studies and workshops. It also links to a "Yurok Cultural Fire Stewardship Plan." A notice on the site says Sudden Oak Death on the upper reservation means no fall Training Exchange this year, and that CAL FIRE suspended burning in Humboldt County as of June 15, 2026.

CFMC works on stewardship and land management, not title. This record found no land returned to or acquired by CFMC.
