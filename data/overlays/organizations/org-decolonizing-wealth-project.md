Decolonizing Wealth Project is a project, not a stand-alone charity. Its donate page says it is fiscally sponsored by Assets Under Movement, EIN 82-1517696, meaning that group holds the legal and tax status. ProPublica lists Assets Under Movement in Washington, DC, with tax exemption issued in September 2017. The about page names Edgar Villanueva (Lumbee) as founder and CEO and says it works with people who hold wealth, to move money toward repair.

Its California Tribal Land Return project launched in 2025. Its page says it gives technical assistance, training and coaching to California tribes working to overcome barriers to land return, including barriers in financing and the law. The project page reports "Acreage Returned in 2025" of 17k+ and 30 tribes represented, with $2.35 million granted. Another page on the same site says the project supports 31 California tribes seeking the return of more than 25,000 acres.

Its Indigenous Earth Fund lists 2026 grantee partners including the Indigenous Environmental Network, Hopi Tutskwa Permaculture Institute and Native Conservancy, and reports $6.3 million granted to organizations and tribes.

The group funds other organizations' work. It does not state that it holds title to land. The acreage figures are its own, and it does not tie them to specific parcels.
