First Nations Development Institute is a national nonprofit based in Longmont, Colorado. ProPublica lists it under EIN 54-1254491, with tax exemption issued in August 1984. Its staff and board page lists Michael E. Roberts (Tlingit) as president and CEO.

Its stated mission is "to uplift and sustain the lifeways and economies of Native communities through advocacy, financial support, and knowledge sharing." It works largely as a funder and intermediary. Its home page says that through year-end 2025 it had managed 4,405 grants totaling over $110.6 million to Native American projects and organizations in 46 states, the District of Columbia and two territories.

On land, its Stewarding Native Lands program began in 2020. The program page says First Nations supports tribes and Native communities in "regaining and restoring their lands" through five focus areas, and lists a Tribal Lands Conservation Fund. The program is directed by Jacque Demko (Mandan, Hidatsa, and Arikara Nation). Grants shown on the page include support for a Northern Cheyenne Tribe buffalo program.

First Nations also describes ratings from charity watchdogs on its About page. Those are its own claims. The organization funds land work; it does not itself hold land for return.
