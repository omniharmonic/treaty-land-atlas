The Great Basin Water Network (GBWN) is a nonprofit based in Baker, Nevada. ProPublica lists it as Great Basin Water Network Inc under EIN 35-2278153, with tax exemption issued in August 2007. Its site says it was formed to protect the water resources of the Great Basin for residents, animals and plants. This is a water group, not a land-return organization.

GBWN's archive page describes its opposition to the Southern Nevada Water Authority's groundwater project: a 300-mile pipeline to move water from east-central Nevada and Utah's west desert to Las Vegas, projected to cost more than $15 billion. The archive says the project was initiated in 1989 and ended in 2020.

Its current campaigns include "Step Up for Steptoe Valley," against a pumped-hydropower proposal, and opposition to a Cedar City pipeline. The board page lists Tamra Borchardt-Slayton, chairwoman of the Indian Peaks Band of the Paiute Tribe of Utah, and Rick and Delaine Spilsbury, members of the Ely Shoshone Tribe. The site thanks the Ely Shoshone, the Confederated Tribes of the Goshute Reservation and the Duckwater Shoshone for their support.

The home page says the Network is seeking an executive director; the former director, Kyle Roerink, is listed as senior advisor.
