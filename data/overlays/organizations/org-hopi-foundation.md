The Hopi Foundation says it was established in 1985 by local Hopis who wanted to promote self-sufficiency and local self-determination, and incorporated as an Arizona 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 1987. ProPublica lists The Hopi Foundation in Kykotsmovi, Arizona, under EIN 74-2488628, with tax exemption issued in May 1988. Its staff directory lists Monica Nuvamsa as interim executive director and gives clan and village for each staff member.

The foundation says it serves a population of 7,000 Hopi across 12 villages. Its model, as it describes it, is that a village or community presents a need, the foundation supplies technical expertise to build a demonstration project, and the process is documented so other villages and tribes can use it. It says it has initiated, grown and housed nearly 20 projects in culture, well-being, capacity building, sustainability, farming, historical preservation and youth development.

Current projects named on its About page include KUYI 88.1 FM Hopi Radio, the HOPI Substance Abuse Prevention Center, the Hopi Leadership Program and the Natwani Coalition. It also makes small community grants.

None of these pages describes buying or returning land. The foundation's connection to land is through farming and sustainability work in Hopi communities.
