The InterTribal Sinkyone Wilderness Council is a Tribal non-profit consortium of ten federally recognized Northern California tribal nations with cultural connections to Sinkyone and neighboring territories, according to its own site. Its member tribes include the Cahto Tribe of Laytonville Rancheria, Round Valley Indian Tribes and several Pomo tribes. ProPublica lists it in Ukiah under EIN 68-0192460, with tax exemption issued in May 1993.

In January 2022, Save the Redwoods League announced that it had transferred ownership of a 523-acre redwood forest on the Lost Coast of Mendocino County to the Council. The League had bought the property, formerly called Andersonia West, in July 2020, then donated it, and the Council granted the League a conservation easement. The Council renamed the forest Tc'ih-Leh-Dun, meaning "Fish Run Place" in the Sinkyone language. KQED quotes Council chairwoman Priscilla Hunter: "It's not often that you get land donated back to the Indians."

The Council's site says its work includes acquisition, lasting protection and cultural care of lands and waters in its traditional territories, and it describes the lands it protects as "nearly 5000 acres." That total is the Council's own figure. The Council says it works to re-establish local tribal peoples' relationship with traditional territories, protect coastal forest and marine ecosystems, and advocate for traditional cultural land and water rights.
