The Isle de Jean Charles Band of Biloxi-Chitimacha-Choctaw is a tribe on the Louisiana coast. ProPublica lists the band's nonprofit, Isle De Jean Charles Band Of Biloxi-chitimacha-choctaw Inc, in Montegut, Louisiana, under EIN 27-0361281, with tax exemption issued in January 2010. The band's donate page says it holds 501(c)(3) status. It has been described as a state-recognized tribe.

The band's resettlement page says its ancestral island is "rapidly eroding." It says that after the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers realigned the Morganza to the Gulf levee in 2001, leaving the island out, the Tribal Council decided to resettle. The page says the band has worked toward resettlement for nearly twenty years and hopes to reunite displaced members and rekindle traditional life-ways.

The band describes its plan as including housing for members at risk of flooding, a community center, museum, gathering areas, childcare and educational spaces, community gardens and a seed-saving program. It lists partners and recognition, including a Mississippi-Alabama Sea Grant award in 2016 and a collaboration with the Grounded Solutions Network on permanently affordable housing.

The band's donate page currently asks for support for Hurricane Ida recovery and other programs. These are the band's own plans, and it describes no completed land transfer.
