Kulāiwi Land Trust is a young nonprofit in Haleʻiwa, Oʻahu. ProPublica lists it under EIN 92-2383824, with tax exemption issued in November 2023. Its own site prints the same tax ID and says it is a nonprofit.

Its mission, as it states it, is "to be a catalyst and a receptacle for the return of Native Hawaiian lands to Native Hawaiians for Native Hawaiian use in the areas of culture, education, health, and wellbeing." Its site lists Kawika Burgess as president, Kamanaʻopono Crabbe as vice president, and Kaliko Amona as executive director. The pages do not state the officers' tribal or ancestral affiliations.

A grant profile at the Hawaiʻi People's Fund describes the group as building "a community-rooted vehicle for the return of 'āina to lāhui." The fund says its work evolved from pursuing a single large project to developing the leadership, strategy and partnerships needed to receive, hold and activate land for community good.

No land that Kulāiwi holds, has received, or has been promised is documented on these pages. There are no acreage figures or property names. This is an organization stating a goal rather than reporting a return.
