The Maidu Summit Consortium (MSC) is a nonprofit based in Chester, California, in the Mountain Maidu homeland. ProPublica lists it under EIN 27-1129385, with tax exemption issued in February 2010. Its board page lists members by group, including Tsi-Akim Maidu, United Maidu Nation, Big Meadow Historical Preservation, Susanville Indian Rancheria, Maidu Cultural Development Group and the Roundhouse Council. Ben Cunningham is listed as chairman.

MSC's mission statement says it is "to preserve, protect, and promote the Mountain Maidu Homeland with a united voice." It says MSC envisions re-acquired ancestral lands as "a vast and unique park system" dedicated to education, healing, protection and ecosystem management based on Maidu cultural and philosophic perspectives.

Its home page lists "Historical Dates To Remember" for land: Land Back on September 20, 2019; Tásmam Koyóm on December 27, 2019; the Maidu Trail on March 5, 2020; the Lake Almanor Maidu Forest on March 24, 2021; and the Lake Almanor Maidu Wetlands on April 14, 2022. It also lists the first beaver conservation release in 75 years from state agencies to MSC property in October 2023, a first Maidu work crew in May 2024, and a property purchase in Westwood in November 2024.

The page gives no acreage, no seller names, and no description of the legal mechanism for each date.
