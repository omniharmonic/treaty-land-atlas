The Miwok Archeological Preserve of Marin (MAPOM) says that since 1970 it has worked to promote accurate knowledge of the Coast Miwok Indians, the first people of Marin and southern Sonoma counties. It says it was founded as a result of community involvement in the excavation of a Coast Miwok village site. ProPublica lists it in Fairfax, California, under EIN 23-7100329. Its support page gives a Fairfax post office box and says MAPOM is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

MAPOM says it works through programs and cooperative efforts with groups including the Federated Indians of Graton Rancheria, the Coast Miwok Tribal Council of Marin and the Point Reyes National Seashore. Its stated mission is to identify, preserve and protect anthropological resources concerning the Coast Miwok and their territory, and to support other Native people through publishing, exhibits, public events and education.

Its home page shows a photo timeline of events at a Coast Miwok cultural site from 1978 to 2023, including roundhouse construction, work days and a Day of Indigenous Skills in 2023.

MAPOM describes itself as an organization that supports Native people. It is listed here as an allied, non-Native nonprofit, and it describes no land purchase or return.
