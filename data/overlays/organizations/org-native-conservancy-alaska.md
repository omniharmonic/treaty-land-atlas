Native Conservancy is a nonprofit based in Cordova, Alaska, on the homeland of the Eyak people. Its own website says it was founded in 2003 and describes itself as an Indigenous-led organization working across land, ocean and community. The site lists Dune Lankard, Eyak Athabaskan, as President and Founder.

The organization groups its work under three names. LandBack programs, in its words, protect ancestral lands "through Native-led trusts and partnerships." OceanBack programs build regenerative ocean farms, including a Native Sea Trust meant to secure long-term access to ocean farming sites for Alaska Native coastal communities. PeopleBack programs focus on education, training and local economies.

The land pages the organization publishes describe programs and priorities, not completed transfers. They cover a planned Eyak totem pole carving and a wild-foods program that delivers harvested foods to Elders. Those pages do not state how many acres Native Conservancy holds or has returned, or who holds title.

IRS records shown by ProPublica list Native Conservancy of Cordova, Alaska as a 501(c)(3) with its tax exemption issued in May 2003. Donors should know that the website's LandBack language describes aims and partnerships. A reader who wants a specific acreage or deed should ask the organization directly.
