Native Governance Center describes itself as a Native-led nonprofit based in St. Paul, Minnesota, that supports tribes in strengthening their sovereignty. It does not buy or return land. Its work is on the governments and leaders who would manage land and other rights.

The center says it opened its doors in January 2016 after tribal leaders from 23 Native nations in Minnesota, North Dakota and South Dakota asked for an organization that could meet demand for governance and leadership resources. It has since added the four Native nations that share geography with Nebraska. Its team page lists Amber Annis, from the Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe, as Executive Director.

Its programs, as listed on its site, are leadership development through a Native Nation Rebuilders program, tribal governance support, tribal finance, and community engagement. For 2025 it reports that over 50 community members from three Native nations took part in candidate forums it moderated, and that 14 changemakers logged 17,142 participation hours, adding to a network of over 200 Rebuilders.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists the organization as a 501(c)(3) in Minneapolis with its tax exemption issued in April 2016. Those figures are the center's own reports, and this entry has not checked them independently.
