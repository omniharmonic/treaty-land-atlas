Native Movement is a Fairbanks, Alaska, organization. Its history page says it was founded in 2003 out of Arctic Village, Alaska. Its first campaign aimed to help build a collective Alaska Native voice for recognition of Indigenous hunting and fishing rights. By 2007 it was formally incorporated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Its mission statement says it is "dedicated to building people power, rooted in an Indigenized worldview, toward healthy, sustainable, & just communities for ALL." The organization says it works through three baskets: community education, action and advocacy, and movement infrastructure support. Its staff page lists Enei Begaye as Executive Director.

Its land role is advocacy, not buying or returning land. Posts on its site include a call to submit a public comment to protect the Yukon Flats and a post headed "Yukon Flats Not For Sale." Its donate page says gifts support its programs and its Community Affiliate Collective, and that donors can name a project in the check memo.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists Native Movement of Fairbanks as a 501(c)(3), with its tax exemption issued in June 2007. This account reflects what the organization publishes about itself and does not assess outcomes of its campaigns.
