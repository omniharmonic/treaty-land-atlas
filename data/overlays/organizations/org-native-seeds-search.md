Native Seeds/SEARCH is a seed conservation nonprofit in Tucson, Arizona. Its About page says it was founded in 1983 after farmers, gardeners, Indigenous community members and conservationists worried about the loss of seed diversity. It now conserves more than 1,800 regional seed varieties in a climate-controlled seed bank.

This is a seed organization, not a land trust. Its mission is to conserve and share the seeds of the people of the desert Southwest and Mexico. The site does not describe the organization as Native-led. It says its seed-bank advisory circle consists of community gardeners, farmers, and Indigenous people connected to the collection. The staff page lists Alexandra Zamecnik as Interim Executive Director, and the board page lists a chair, vice chair and eleven members.

Its programs include Community Seed Grants, a Native American Seed Share that gives a limited number of seed packets at no or reduced cost to Native American individuals in the Southwest, and a Partner Farmer Program that favors Indigenous farmers. It also describes a formal rematriation process: "the return of seed to a person or tribe with a direct relationship to those seeds."

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists the group, under its longer legal name, as a Tucson 501(c)(3) with tax exemption issued in October 1983.
