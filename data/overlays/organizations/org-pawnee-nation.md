The Pawnee Nation of Oklahoma's website says the Nation has a history spanning more than 2000 years and is made up of four bands: the Chawi (Grand), Kitkahaki (Republican), Pitahawirata (Tappage) and Skidi (Wolf). Its Government page says the Pawnee Business Council is the supreme governing body and exercises the inherent, statutory, treaty and self-government powers of the Nation. A 2026 constitution convention is considering revisions to the Nation's constitution.

On land and resources, the Nation's Division of Natural Resources and Safety says it is dedicated to conservation, protection, management and use of the tribe's natural resources. The Department of Environmental Conservation and Safety, established in 1997, is developing water quality, safe drinking water, fish and wildlife, solid waste and other programs with U.S. Environmental Protection Agency assistance. The Pawnee Nation Historic Preservation Office monitors sites on the cultural landscape and works within the boundaries of the original Pawnee Indian Reservation.

A land return in Nebraska is described by outside sources. A 2008 Native American Rights Fund item says writer Roger Welsch gifted 60 acres in Nebraska back to the Pawnee Nation. A 2026 KPBS documentary notice refers to the Pawnee's return to their Nebraska homelands through a writer's land donation. The Nation's own pages cited here do not describe it.
