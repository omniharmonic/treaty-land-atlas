Peace Development Fund (PDF) is a public foundation in Amherst, Massachusetts. Its history page says it was founded in June 1981 by Bob Mazer and Meg Gage to resource grassroots peace and disarmament organizing. It makes grants, advises donors and fiscally sponsors grassroots groups. Its FY2025 annual report lists Allistair Mallillin as Executive Director and says it distributed $2.4 million in grants to 181 grantees.

PDF does not claim to be Indigenous-led. Its Land Back page frames Land Back as a national movement to restore Indigenous lands, with philanthropists playing a financing role. It says Paul Haible, executive director from 2004 to 2024, worked with a donor-advised-fund donor to fund land back projects from the sale of the donor's family farm in Iowa. To mark his retirement, PDF created the Haible Land Back Fund, to connect resources to Indigenous grassroots groups for programs that move or restore lands. The page says PDF is seeking to build an endowment for it. That is a fundraising goal, not a result.

The one concrete grant the page names is to Chickaloon Village Traditional Council, which received funding to explore wind and solar projects on Chickaloon Tribal land. PDF says its support helped leverage another $7 million, a figure from PDF itself.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists Peace Development Fund Inc as a 501(c)(3), tax exemption issued May 1982.
