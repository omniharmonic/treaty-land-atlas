The Pocasset Pokanoket Land Trust (PPLT) calls itself a tribally-led nonprofit dedicated to reconnecting American Indian tribes, clans, Urban Indians and Indigenous people across the Northeast. Its staff and board pages list Chief Sequan Pijaki as Executive Director and Founder and as Chairman. Its tax exemption was issued in September 2017, according to ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, which lists it as a 501(c)(3) in Cranston, Rhode Island.

PPLT's initiatives page lists six lines of work, with a stated purpose of putting ancestral land back in Indigenous hands. Under Land Repatriation, the objective is to reclaim ancestral lands for tribal stewardship. The status line reads that it currently stewards 50 acres of ancestral land in Tiverton, Rhode Island, and names Bally's Tiverton, the Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management, the Island Foundation and the Indigenous Peoples Network as partners. The page does not say who holds title to those acres or how they were obtained.

Other programs include a farmer and land steward support program that offers one-on-one help with grants, business planning and soil health, and an Indigenous Food Sovereignty micro-farming program led by Rocky Johnson, a Narragansett Indian, which PPLT fiscally sponsors.

This entry reports PPLT's own descriptions. The organization's self-description as tribally led was not checked against any outside source.
