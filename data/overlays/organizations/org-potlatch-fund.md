Potlatch Fund describes itself as a Native-led intermediary that supports Native individuals, Native-led organizations, over 50 Native nations and over 225 Alaska Native Tribal communities. Its history page says it began as a tribally driven organization founded in December 2002 by Northwest tribal leaders to expand philanthropy in Northwest Indian Country.

It is a grantmaker, not a land trust. Its programs page lists two grant programs. Seasonal grants of up to $2,500 support Native seasonal activities. The Power of Ceremony and Healing Grant supports larger ceremonial, cultural and community practices. Its Our Impact page says it has given more than $11 million in gifts since 2002, about 200 gifts a year, with more than $500,000 in grants each year. These are Potlatch Fund's own figures.

The About page names its board, with tribal affiliations given. It lists Jordann Lankford (A'aniiih and Anishinaabe) as President, Meredith Parker (Makah) as Vice President, and tai simpson (Nimiipuu) as Secretary.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists Potlatch Fund of Seattle as a 501(c)(3) with tax exemption issued in October 2004. A donor should know that a gift supports a grantmaking program for Native communities generally. The sources here do not describe any land acquisition or return by Potlatch Fund.
