Right Relationship Boulder (RRB) is a Boulder, Colorado community group. Its Land Group page names its founders as Jerilyn DeCoteau and Paula Palmer. It does not state that its leadership is Native. Donations go through a fiscal sponsor, Mediators Foundation, which ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists as a Boulder 501(c)(3) with exemption issued in February 1988. The page asks donors to select "Land Group Projects" or write "RRB Land Group" on a check memo.

The Land Group says it collaborates with Indigenous peoples to broaden their access to lands in the Boulder Valley and to make sure they have input on land-use decisions. Its first goal is to support Native American tribes that were forcibly removed from Colorado's Front Range so they can reestablish a presence there. It raises funds and logistics for tribes to bring youths to their Front Range homelands. For 2026 it is raising money for an Arapaho Unity Youth Gathering for Arapaho youth and elders from Wyoming and Oklahoma, with a $30,000 goal.

A second goal is that the Fort Chambers site in Boulder be developed and managed with guidance from the Arapaho and Cheyenne nations. RRB has published a statement on a decision by the city's Open Space and Mountain Parks department.

The sources here describe no land return or acquisition. The fundraising figure is a goal.
