Save the Redwoods League is a California conservation nonprofit that describes itself as an independent nonprofit working across the state's redwood range. It is not Indigenous-led. Its leadership page lists Steve Mietz as President and CEO. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists it as a 501(c)(3) with tax exemption issued in December 1921. The league says it has protected more than 220,000 acres of redwood forests.

Its story about 'O Rew, written by a freelance writer and posted on its site on July 28, 2026, says the league bought a defunct mill site near Orick, California, in 2013. The site covers 125 acres at the southern entrance to Redwood National and State Parks. In 2021 the league, the Yurok Tribe and partners began a five-year restoration of Prairie Creek on the site. The story says the league transferred the 'O Rew Redwoods Gateway to the Yurok Tribe on June 29, 2026, for permanent protection and public access.

The Yurok Tribe's own home page also announces the land transfer, quoting Chairman Joseph L. James and Mr. Mietz. The tribe's page gives the date of conveyance as June 30, 2026.

The pages read do not state a sale price or whether the Tribe paid for the land.
