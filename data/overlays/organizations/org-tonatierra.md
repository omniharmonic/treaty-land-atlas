Tonatierra Community Development Institute is a Phoenix, Arizona nonprofit. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists it as a 501(c)(3) with exemption issued in June 1997. Its website says its mission is to create and sustain a Cultural Embassy of the Indigenous Peoples that will support local-global community development in education, culture and economic development, in accord with principles of community ecology and self-determination. It calls the embassy Nahuacalli.

Its History page says the families of Tonatierra develop decolonization strategies that support a movement for environmental justice and self-determination as Indigenous Peoples at local, regional, continental and global levels. The page pictures projects such as the Xinachtli youth cultural education project and events involving the United Nations Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues.

The home page describes a continental effort it calls Superseding the Doctrine of Discovery, tied to a 2013 conference at Arizona State University West and a Continental Commission Abya Yala. The site says it organizes community life through a Calpolli, a kinship system of extended families.

Its pages describe no land purchase, return or acreage, and name no current leaders.
