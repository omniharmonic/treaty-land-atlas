The Trust for Public Land (TPL) is a national conservation nonprofit. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists it as a 501(c)(3) with exemption issued in February 1978. What follows comes from news coverage.

Tribal Business News reported on November 4, 2023 that TPL had secured more than 31,000 acres in Maine's Katahdin region with plans to return them to the Penobscot people. The article says TPL bought the land in 2022 from Conservation Resources, LLC, for $29.5 million, and was hoping to raise $32 million to repay loans taken out for the purchase and return the acreage to the Penobscot Nation. It also quotes the Penobscot Nation's natural resources director on the value of access to a national monument. The article names Ken Lucero, an enrolled member of the Pueblo of Zia, as TPL's Tribal and Indigenous Lands Director.

Maine Public, on June 26, 2026, named Trust for Public Land as one of the partners in the Tributary Land Returns with the five federally recognized Wabanaki tribes.

Whether the Penobscot return has been completed was not confirmed from the sources here. TPL's broader tribal-lands program claims could not be verified.
