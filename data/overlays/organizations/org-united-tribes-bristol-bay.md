United Tribes of Bristol Bay (UTBB) describes itself as a Tribal consortium. Its About page says its membership is 14 federally recognized Tribes in Bristol Bay, representing over 80 percent of the region's population, and that Bristol Bay Tribes founded it in 2013 to have a united voice in efforts to protect their way of life. Its board is made up of representatives from each of the 14 member Tribal governments. Its staff page lists Alannah Hurley (Yup'ik) as Executive Director.

UTBB calls itself a political division of its member Tribal governments. It names four focus areas: long-term guardianship of lands, air and waters; tribal self-determination and sovereignty; sustainable economic development; and community wellbeing. Its lands page says that, together with partners, it has fought the proposed Pebble mine for two decades and works toward permanent protections for land and water and a role for Tribes in managing those lands.

Its sovereignty page says that work includes building capacity through education, self-governance tools and traditional justice systems, and describes this work as still developing.

Its pages describe no land purchase or return. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer has no listing under the organization's name. Its donate page says it is recognized to receive tax-deductible contributions.
