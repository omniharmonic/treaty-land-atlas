The Wabanaki Alliance says it was formed in June 2020 by the tribes in Maine: the Mi'kmaq Nation, the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians, the Passamaquoddy Tribe and the Penobscot Nation. Its stated purpose is to educate people in Maine about the need for securing the sovereignty of the tribes in Maine. Its staff page lists Maulian Bryant, a citizen of the Penobscot Nation, as Executive Director.

The Alliance's Who We Are page ties its work to the 1980 Maine Indian Claims Settlement Act. It says the tribes agreed to that Act under political duress, that the promise to improve it was never fulfilled, and that the Act has stripped Maine's tribes of sovereignty, leaving them treated as less than other federally recognized tribes. These are the Alliance's positions. Its Tribal Lands page explains reservation, trust and fee land in Maine and offers a map.

Its recent activity, per its home page, includes a 2026 legislative scorecard for the 132nd Maine Legislature and election endorsements. It also runs a youth policy initiative.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer lists the Wabanaki Alliance of Stillwater, Maine, as a 501(c)(4) civic league, exemption issued November 2020. Its donate page states that contributions are not tax-deductible.
