The Yurok Tribe's website says its mission is to exercise the aboriginal and sovereign rights of the Yurok People to continue tribal traditions of self-governance, cultural and spiritual preservation, and stewardship of Yurok lands, waters and other natural endowments. Joseph L. James is quoted on the site as Chairman of the Yurok Tribe.

Its newsletter archive lists land headlines. The June 2025 edition carries "Tribe Regains Ownership of Blue Creek." Western Rivers Conservancy, the nonprofit that worked with the Tribe, says on its own site that it and the Tribe set up a long-term partnership in 2009 to buy 47,097 acres along the lower Klamath River and Blue Creek, and that in May 2025 they completed the largest single "land back" deal in California history, creating the Yurok Tribal Community Forest.

The tribe's home page also announces a 2026 transfer. A July 28, 2026 press release says Save the Redwoods League transferred 125 acres in the heart of Yurok ancestral territory back to the Tribe, and that the property was conveyed on June 30, 2026. The 'O Rew Redwoods Gateway is a tribally owned and operated recreation area at the southern end of Redwood National and State Parks, open to the public from August 1, 2026.

The Tribe's own pages read show no donation page. A gift to Western Rivers Conservancy or Save the Redwoods League does not go to the Tribe.
