On July 28, 2026 the Washington Post published an article, headlined "Their land was taken centuries ago. Now the Pamunkey tribe is getting it back," about the Pamunkey Indian Tribe of Virginia regaining a piece of its ancestral homeland. The newspaper also shared the story in a social media post.

That post says the land in King William County, Virginia, was taken from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe centuries ago. It says that through a recent $1.9 million deal the tribe regained ownership and plans to expand its cultural and economic presence in the region. The article's web address refers to the tribe's plan to use 13 acres for cultural and educational purposes.

A Long & Foster release says the tribe bought Windsor Shades, a 13-acre estate overlooking the Pamunkey River near West Point, for $1.95 million in May. It does not say how the purchase was financed or what the tribe will build.
