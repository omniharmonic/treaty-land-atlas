On July 13, 2026, President Trump signed Proclamation 11043, modifying Bears Ears, and Proclamation 11044, modifying Grand Staircase-Escalante. Both were published in the Federal Register on July 17, 2026.

Proclamation 11043 says the 2021 designation, which expanded the monument to about 1.36 million acres, "suffers from several flaws under Antiquities Act analysis." It says proper care can be provided "by a smaller reservation of approximately 121,096 acres comprising two units: the Shash Jáa Unit and the Indian Creek Unit," and that approximately 1,238,904 acres are excluded. Those excluded lands "shall be open to" entry under the public land laws, to mineral and geothermal leasing, and to location under the mining laws. The proclamation also says the Bears Ears Commission "is hereby disbanded and terminated," while Interior and Agriculture "shall continue to consult with Tribal Nations in accordance with other applicable authorities." It creates an advisory committee with one seat each for the Hopi, Navajo, Ute Indian, Ute Mountain Ute and Zuni governments.

Proclamation 11044 says Grand Staircase-Escalante had been increased to about 1.87 million acres in 2021 and substitutes a reservation of approximately 181,541 acres in two units.

The Bears Ears Inter-Tribal Coalition's site calls the Bears Ears change "one of the most severe setbacks in the history of Bears Ears" and says it ended "the nation's first and most established framework for Tribal collaborative management of a national monument." Whether courts will uphold the proclamations was not known from the sources here.
