In December 2023 the Interior Department published a final rule rewriting the regulations that carry out NAGPRA, at 43 CFR part 10. The Federal Register notice appeared on December 13, 2023, and the rule took effect January 12, 2024.

Its summary says it "revises and replaces definitions and procedures for lineal descendants, Indian Tribes, Native Hawaiian organizations, museums, and Federal agencies." The rule sets out a step-by-step roadmap with specific timelines for museums and federal agencies to carry out repatriation. And, throughout, museums and agencies "must defer to the Native American traditional knowledge of lineal descendants, Indian Tribes, and Native Hawaiian organizations."

The preamble lists several changes made in response to public comment. It added a requirement of free, prior, and informed consent before any exhibition of, access to, or research on human remains or cultural items. And it extended the timeline to allow five years, rather than two as proposed, for museums and agencies to consult and update their inventories of human remains and associated funerary objects.

The record also shows the rule was contested. In discussing the purpose paragraph, the Department reported that four comments specifically objected to including deference. Those comments expressed concern about how deference applies when tribes disagree.
