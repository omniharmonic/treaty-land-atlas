This is context, not an adjudicated treaty violation. Bears Ears National Monument in Utah is a national monument created and changed by presidential proclamation, not by treaty. This entry tracks how its size has changed since the 2016 designation.

Archaeology Southwest reports that President Biden's October 2021 proclamation was "redesignating and expanding Bears Ears National Monument" after President Trump's 2017 reduction. On July 13, 2026, President Trump signed a new proclamation. Archaeology Southwest says it cut the monument from about 1.35 million acres to 121,096 acres. Hoodline reports the cuts could open land with known coal, uranium and other mineral deposits to leasing and extraction.

Conservation groups call the move illegal and are asking a federal court to reopen a case first filed in 2017 so they can challenge it. Archaeology Southwest's Skylar Begay said, "Downsizing a national monument was illegal in 2017, and it is illegal in 2026." Whether a president may shrink a monument is a legal question not decided in these sources.
