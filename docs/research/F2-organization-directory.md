See `verification/CORRECTIONS-APPLIED.md` for each change and for what was not applied. No lawyer or tribal government has reviewed this file.*

**Read this first.**

- "Verified" means a page or registry record was read this session. Fetches were made through a summarizing tool; EINs were taken from ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer search/organization pages and, where noted, from the organization's own page. A human reviewer should re-confirm any EIN against IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search before the site shows it (the brief's verification agent will do this).
- Charity Navigator checks were made at `charitynavigator.org/ein/<EIN>`. Candid/GuideStar was not accessible this session.
- "Indigenous-led" is reported two ways: *self-described* (the organization says so) and *verified* (leadership named with tribal affiliation in a source I read). Where I could not read either, it is `unverified`.
- Nothing in this file is a recommendation to give to a particular group; the site's own donation policy (F4) governs what is shown.

## 1. Indigenous-led national organizations and campaigns

### 1.1 NDN Collective (LANDBACK Campaign)  `Tier 1`
- **Official name / type:** NDN Collective (legal name "Ndn Collective Inc"), 501(c)(3). LANDBACK is a campaign of NDN Collective, hosted at landback.org.
- **Indigenous-led:** Self-described ("an Indigenous-led organization dedicated to building Indigenous power") `[verified]` on ndncollective.org/about. Leadership names were not on the page read.
- **Territory / nations:** Pan-Indigenous; based in Rapid City, SD (Oceti Sakowin territory).
- **Work:** LANDBACK Campaign; cornerstone: closing Mount Rushmore and returning Black Hills public lands `[verified]` (landback.org/donate text). NDN Collective's other program areas were not reviewed.
- **Donate:** https://ndncollective.org/donate/ (card, Apple Pay, Venmo, PayPal, bank; check by mail to 408 Knollwood Dr, Rapid City, SD 57701; stock gifts by email) `[verified]`.
- **Registry:** EIN 82-3776329; IRS-exempt since June 2018; Form 990 for FY2024 filed Oct. 2025 with revenue about $12.6M (ProPublica); Charity Navigator 4/4 stars.
- **Caveat:** The donate page does not describe a dedicated LANDBACK fund, and landback.org/donate has no donation form or EIN text in the version read. Before the site says "give to LANDBACK," email NDN Collective to learn how a gift can be designated.
- **Confidence:** high (existence, EIN); medium (fund designation).

## 8. Non-monetary ways to support (and how to do it respectfully)

1. **Pay voluntary rent or land tax, where the host people ask for it.** Sogorea Te' (Shuumi Land Tax, East Bay) and Real Rent Duwamish (Seattle) are explicit asks from the Indigenous host organizations `[verified]`. Pay where you live; do not invent new "taxes" or collect them yourself.
2. **Offer what the organization asks for in kind.** Sogorea Te's donation page lists goods (building supplies, plant starts, solar equipment, vehicles), REI points, housing referrals and land offers; it links a "Return Land" page for people with land `[verified]`.
3. **Return land you hold.** Contact an Indigenous-led land trust or the tribe whose territory it is. Native Land Conservancy offers Cultural Respect Easements as one structure `[verified]`. Do not transfer to an intermediary without the tribe's input.
4. **Show up as asked.** Volunteer with tribal youth camps and gatherings through Right Relationship Boulder's Land Group (Arapaho and Cheyenne, at the tribes' request) `[verified]`.
5. **Advocacy tied to live decisions.** Fort Reno (H.R. 7567 would make the USDA disposal bar permanent; Cheyenne and Arapaho Tribes seek return) `[verified]`; the Menominee Legend Lake buybacks; Pe' Sla drilling; the Maine Tributary Land Returns funding campaign. Always verify the current bill status before telling people to call.
6. **Use and share tribal sources.** Cite the nations' own pages (Taos Pueblo's Blue Lake account; Esselen, Yurok and other tribal websites) and name places with their Indigenous names.
7. **Learn the legal history** (this atlas), and fund Indigenous-led legal work (NARF; NHLC in Hawaii).
8. **What not to do:** do not "rematriate" land by informally handing it to a friend or group claiming descent; do not visit or publicize sacred sites unless the steward invites the public; do not crowdfund "for" a tribe without its written consent.
