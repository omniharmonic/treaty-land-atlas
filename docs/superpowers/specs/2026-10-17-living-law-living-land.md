# Living Law, Living Land

Approved brief: the user chose this exact name, overlapping contour identity, warmer pastel earth tones, and the proposed explorable treaty experience. They authorized implementation in this session for a launch tomorrow.

Keep the clean typography and generous layout. Use oat #f5f1e8, paper #fdfbf6, sage #dfe7d6, clay #eddcd3, ochre #eee2bd, and deep olive #44543c. Text must meet WCAG AA; pastel surfaces are not text colors. The contour mark is an original abstract pair of overlapping organic boundaries, with no borrowed cultural motifs.

Treaty discovery needs search, region and ratification filtering, useful entry points, and readable dated cards. Every treaty keeps its existing route. Treaty detail needs a short orientation, published source-based narrative and promises where available, a selectable chronology beside actual recorded geometry, linked nations and legal context, and prominent organizations. Every event links to its full record and evidence. Unknown dates stay unknown, context is not a finding of breach, unratified instruments are labeled, proposed edges remain suggested, and missing geometry or narrative has a usable fallback. A selected event may highlight only geometry explicitly connected to that event; never invent time-based territorial reconstruction from a violation date. No source data, eligibility rules, infrastructure, publication gates, or payment handling is to be replaced.

Verify unit data transformations, keyboard and pointer interactions, deep links and back navigation, reduced motion, mobile overflow, automated accessibility, existing map/place flows, production build, lint and typecheck. This work is local implementation, not a request to deploy.
