# V1 Legal Citation Verification Report

Verification Agent V1, independent of the research agents. Scope: legal citations in `seed-data/cases-seed.csv` (104 rows) and `seed-data/statutes-seed.csv` (51 rows), plus the high-consequence items named for `docs/research/C1`, `B1`, `B2`. No seed or research file was edited. Machine-readable changes are in `V1-corrections.csv`.

## 1. Method and limits

- Every fetch was a direct `WebFetch` of a Justia, Cornell LII, govinfo (Statutes at Large PDFs), Federal Register, American Presidency Project, National Archives or Wikipedia URL. Primary sources were preferred. Wikipedia was used for recent reporter page numbers, three lower-court citations (Murphy v. Royal, Cayuga, Navajo Nation v. Forest Service), the Lumbee/NDAA Public Law number and the Sioux fund totals; each such item says so in its note.
- `WebSearch` quota was exhausted. `curl` through the proxy was refused (403), so it was not used. Some pages did not load (govinfo 80 Stat. 1509, 96 Stat. 2517, 94 Stat. 2371, 136 Stat. 840; LoC `usrep` pages; supremecourt.gov bound volumes; CourtListener, Casetext and Case.law are blocked). Where this left a claim open it is marked `could-not-verify`, not guessed.
- Justia and Cornell print only `U.S. ___` for recent opinions (volumes 584 to 599), so reporter page numbers for 2018 to 2023 cases rest on Wikipedia infoboxes. Recommend a one-time check against the bound volumes at supremecourt.gov before release.
- Status vocabulary: **confirmed** (found value matches), **corrected** (found value differs; correction supplied), **could-not-verify**, **wrong** (flatly false). No item rose to **wrong**; the nearest are listed in Section 2.
- One item is one claim. A row can carry several items (for example a citation, a treaty field and a reporter page).

### Most serious findings, in order

1. **Sioux Nation award wording is ambiguous (B2 V024).** The opinion states $17.1 million (land value as of Feb. 28, 1877; $450,000 more for gold; the Court of Claims award is stated as 'at least $17.5 million, without interest') and 5% interest from 1877, but no total. B2 says 'plus 5% interest from 1877, about $105 million then'. Secondary sources (PBS 2011; Wikipedia's arithmetic of roughly $88 million of interest) show $105 million is the approximate **total**, not the interest. A reader could take it as $105 million of interest on top of principal, implying about $122 million, which no source read supports. Clarify in the text and the seed.
2. **City of Sherrill treaty field is wrong in substance.** The seed lists the Treaty of Fort Stanwix (1784); the opinion never mentions it. It discusses the Treaty of Fort Schuyler (1788) and the Treaty of Canandaigua (1794).
3. **Washington v. United States (culverts) cites a superseded Ninth Circuit opinion.** The seed says it affirmed 827 F.3d 836 (9th Cir. 2016). That is the June 27, 2016 opinion; the judgment actually reviewed was the amended opinion of Mar. 2, 2017.
4. **United States v. Dann: the Treaty of Ruby Valley is not in the opinion.** The seed's `treaty_at_issue` is unsupported by the opinion text.
5. **Statute pin errors.** Tribal Law and Order Act (the Act is at 124 Stat. 2258; Title II, the TLOA, starts at 2261) and VAWA 2022 (136 Stat. 49 is the start of the whole omnibus; the tribal provisions are at 136 Stat. 898, div. W, tit. VIII, sec. 804).
6. **Seneca (Kinzua) caption.** The D.C. Circuit caption is Seneca Nation of Indians v. Brucker, 262 F.2d 27 (Nov. 25, 1958), not '... v. United States'.
7. **Smaller field errors:** Carpenter v. Shaw lists Chickasaw allottees (petitioners are Choctaw); Fletcher v. Peck names Creek and Cherokee lands (the opinion names no tribe); Employment Division v. Smith '(Klamath)' and Bay Mills 'Treaty of 1836' are not in the opinions.
8. **Lumbee recognition row lacks a Public Law number;** secondary sources give Pub. L. 119-60 (NDAA FY2026, S. 1071). 'H.R. 474' is unsupported.

### Everything else holds

All 14 `from-memory` statutes have correct Statutes at Large citations (one, the 1851 Indian Appropriations Act, is confirmed only as to start page and subject). Every Supreme Court citation fetched matched, including the ten items named in the task: Sioux Nation, Lone Wolf, the Act of Mar. 3, 1871, the Dawes Act, the Indian Reorganization Act, HCR 108, Public Law 280, McGirt, Castro-Huerta, Brackeen, Sherrill and Tee-Hit-Ton.
